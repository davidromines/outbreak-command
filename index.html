Campaign: you start small with only troops, barricades, gates and safe zones; the military arrives in stage 2, when every weapon becomes available (the nuke in stage 3). Blueprints let you use a tool in stage 1 too. Buildings show who is inside: blue = people sheltering (hover for the count), green = zombies breaking in, dark = empty. Hold each stage until its timer runs out, or wipe out every zombie to contain it. The infection then breaks out into a bigger area grown along the real roads and bridges, until the whole metro is in play. If the share of civilians alive drops below the stage's fall line, the run is over. Each stage pays supply points for how it went (civilians alive, stars, containment, kills). Spend them at the requisition screen between stages on upgrades for the rest of the run, supplies for the next stage, and blueprints that unlock new tools for good. Medals earned in a run buy permanent upgrades. Sandbox mode has everything unlocked. The outbreak's location, size and strain are hidden until you press Start. Zombies come as walkers, bright runners and dark crawlers; they can wade across water (blue-grey, slow, untargetable). Units: blue box = APC, pink ring = turret, orange triangle = mortar. Every unit has limited ammo: grey troops are out and walk back to a safe zone to restock, so build one. Garrisons fire slowly and hold at most 16 troops; when their ammo runs out they abandon the building as civilians. Buildings hit by shells, bombs and missiles, or burnt out by fire, are destroyed: once about a third of a building is gone it collapses, killing anyone inside, and leaves walkable rubble. APCs drive out hunting zombies like troops. The destroyer's deck cannon fires slow, heavy shells on its own. Press P (or hold Shift), then drag a short line to order 100 paratroopers (costs budget; budget grows once the outbreak is live). A transport plane flies the line and they jump after a 6 s countdown; every air order shows its countdown over the target. They land on the nearest open ground and advance on zombies they can't see. B barricade / G gate: drag a line to build (orange blocks everyone, cyan lets humans and troopers through; zombies chew through both). The map is 3D: real terrain heights and buildings. Right-drag (or Ctrl-drag) to rotate and tilt; Reset view puts the camera back. V APC: click to place an armoured vehicle (holds position; select and order it like troops; it can't be infected, runs zombies over, and zombies must wreck it). M mortar squad: click to place; it shells the biggest zombie group within range without needing to see it, but the blast kills anyone in the open, so keep civilians and troops clear. I sniper team: click to place five snipers (violet): very long range, slow fire, need line of sight. Garrison them in a tall building for double the height bonus. T turret: click to place a fixed sentry gun (green square): fast fire, can't be infected or moved, and zombies will come to wreck it. Q airstrike: drag a line and a bomber carpets it a few seconds later, killing everyone in the open and flattening walls. 1 gunship: click a spot and a helicopter circles it for 30 s, gunning zombies from above (a few stray rounds hit civilians beside them). N nuke: click a target; after a short countdown everything within the big ring dies, garrisons included, and fallout keeps killing anything in the open that enters for a while. Air support and nukes only work once the outbreak is live. L (sandbox only) requisition screen. Air strikes (live only): Q carpet bombing, drag a line, bombs scatter wide; E precision bomb, click; R A-10 strafing run, drag a line, a narrow strip of cannon fire that hits civilians too; F firebombing, drag a line, napalm sets buildings on fire and the fire keeps spreading, killing garrisons and anyone beside a burning building. U howitzer: very long range, slow, heavy shells. 2 destroyer: click on water; its gun fires on its own, O sends one of its missiles where you click. J razor wire: drag a line; it slows zombies but stops nobody. Z safe zone: drag to draw any shape, or click point by point and click the first point (or double-click) to close; click a zone to remove it. Civilians gather inside and units restock ammo there. If a zombie gets in it turns red and everyone runs out. K garrison: hover a building to see what it holds and costs, click to fill it with troops who never leave; if zombies break in, half of them turn and come out. Height matters: units higher than their target shoot farther; shooting uphill does less damage. C = command mode: drag a box to select troopers, click open ground to move them or click a building to garrison it (protected, longer range from height; zombies must break down the door, shown by a red ring growing inside the blue one), H hunt, X hold, Ctrl+A select all. W A S D or drag to pan, scroll to zoom, right-drag to tilt/rotate, right-click to drop the current tool or selection. Space pauses.
